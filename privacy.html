Privacy notice

Kabani Safaris client portal · Version 0.1 · 17 November 2026 · Terms of use

This notice explains what personal data we process when you use the Kabani Safaris client portal, why, who else sees it, and how you can use your rights. It follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, for people in the EU or EEA, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). We have tried to keep it short and plain.

1. Who is responsible

Kabani Consulting, trading as Kabani Safaris, 24 Boulevard des Philosophes, 1205 Geneva, Switzerland.
Email: reservations@kabanisafaris.com · WhatsApp: +41 78 611 23 30

2. What data we process

  • To sign you in: your email address, the one-time code we send you, and a sign-in session in your browser (see section 9). There are no passwords.
  • Your trip: your name or names, the trip title and dates, the day-by-day itinerary, your guide, the places you stay, what is included, the price and payment schedule, whether each payment is recorded as received, which bookings are confirmed, and which checklist items you have ticked.
  • Emails we send you from the portal: your email address, the message (for example the sign-in code) and whether it was delivered.
  • Technical data: your IP address, browser type and the time of each request. Our service providers record this automatically to run and protect the service.
  • If you ask for a login but have no trip with us: your email address. We send you one short information email and keep a record of the address for up to 7 days, so that we do not send it twice in one day.

The portal does not ask for passport numbers, payment card or bank details, or health information. The checklist only records whether you ticked an item. Please do not send us this kind of data through the portal.

Most of this data comes from you (when you book or contact us). Trip details and payments are entered by us. Technical data comes from your browser.

5. Data sent abroad

The database is in Switzerland (Zurich). Some processing takes place in other countries:

  • Singapore, the USA and the EU (Supabase): according to Supabase, account information, support requests, usage logs and content delivery caches may be handled outside the Zurich region. Supabase states that its logs are stored in data centres in the EU.
  • USA (Resend): Resend states that its main processing takes place in the USA.
  • USA (GitHub, Google): the page files and our mailbox are handled by US companies that are certified under the Swiss-US Data Privacy Framework (checked on the official register on 17 November 2026).

Where the destination country does not have a data protection level that Switzerland recognises as adequate, we rely on one of two safeguards. For Supabase and Resend we rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses with the Swiss adaptations recognised by the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) (Art. 16(2)(d) FADP): Supabase's data processing addendum includes a Swiss addendum, and Resend's applies the same clauses to transfers from Switzerland. For GitHub and Google we rely on their certification under the Swiss-US Data Privacy Framework, under which Switzerland treats certified US companies as providing adequate protection (Art. 16(1) FADP).

6. How long we keep it

  • Sign-in session: it stays active while you use the portal and for up to 15 minutes after you last used it. After that you are signed out and need a new code. You can also sign out yourself at any time.
  • Your access to the portal: ends 3 months after your trip ends. After that you can no longer see your trip page or sign in to it.
  • Our internal record of your trip: after your access ends we keep a record of the trip in our own administration: your name and email address, the trip, dates, price and payments. We use it for accounting, for statistics about our business and to plan future trips. We keep it for as long as we need it for those purposes and no longer than [10 years after the trip], then delete or anonymise it. If you ask, we delete or anonymise earlier whatever we do not need for accounting.
  • Accounting records of payments: kept for 10 years, as Swiss law requires (Art. 958f of the Code of Obligations).
  • Trip plans as templates: we may reuse a day-by-day itinerary as a starting point for other travellers. Your name and contact details are not part of that.
  • Email address of someone without a trip: up to 7 days.
  • Technical logs: our providers keep them for limited periods that they set.

7. Your rights

You can ask us, free of charge, for:

  • Access to the data we hold about you (Art. 25 FADP). We reply within 30 days.
  • Correction of data that is wrong (Art. 32 FADP).
  • Deletion of your data, or a stop to a particular use, where the law allows it (Art. 32 FADP). We may have to keep some records, for example accounting records.
  • A copy of the data you gave us in a common electronic format, or its transfer to another provider (Art. 28 FADP).

People in the EU or EEA have further rights under the GDPR, including to object to processing based on our legitimate interest.

To use any of these rights, write to reservations@kabanisafaris.com. We may ask you to confirm your identity first. You can also complain to the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), www.edoeb.admin.ch, or, if you live in the EU or EEA, to your local data protection authority.

8. How we protect it

  • All connections are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Each client can see only their own trip. This is enforced by the database itself, not only by the web page.
  • Only Kabani Safaris can create trips and edit them.
  • You sign in with a code sent to your email. The code works once and expires after one hour.
  • Accounts cannot be created by the public. A trip must exist for your email address first.

9. Storage in your browser

When you sign in, the portal stores a sign-in token and the time of your last activity in your browser's local storage, so that you stay signed in for up to 15 minutes after you last used the portal (for example if you visit our website and come back). They are used only for that purpose. The portal uses no advertising, tracking or analytics tools, and loads nothing from other companies' servers, not even fonts. While you are signed in, the portal also sets one small cookie (kb_portal, value "client" or "admin", no personal data) on kabanisafaris.com so that our website can show "My trip" instead of "Client Login"; it expires after the same 15 minutes and is removed when you sign out. You can refuse this storage by not signing in. You can remove it at any time by signing out or by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

We may update this notice, for example when we change a service provider. The date and version at the top show when it last changed. If a change matters to you, we will tell you by email.
